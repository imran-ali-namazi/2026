### 1\. Death as a Natural Deadline

In economics, scarcity creates value. If time were infinite, no moment would be precious. We often view death as a thief, but it acts more like an **editor**. It forces us to choose what matters, to prioritize the people we love, and to find meaning in the "now" because there is no "forever."

### 3\. The "Two Deaths" Concept

There is a common philosophical idea that every person dies twice:

1.  **The Physical Death:** When the body ceases to function.
2.  **The Final Memory:** The last time someone speaks your name or thinks of you. This reminds us that our primary "afterlife" isn't necessarily a mystical place, but the **echoes of our actions** in the lives of those we leave behind.

When we recognize that every person we meet—from the powerful to the vulnerable—is walking toward the same inevitable conclusion, it becomes much harder to treat others with anything but kindness.
