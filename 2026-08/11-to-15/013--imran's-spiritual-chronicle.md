Ten points I gave you but those were in my dreams.

I then awoke in fright, alone - how gut wrenching my screams!

In the Chapel, as God is my witness, I swear

To earn my ten points, live and die for you, I dare.

Teaching me to chip away at myself, tear down 

my defenses.

I guess the real inner work begins when we get out of our comfort zone,

Battered and tattered I feel, maybe for no fault of my own.

Ten points I deserved for springing deep into the chaos of living,

But now that chaos is a cacophony that stops me from singing.

What did I ever do to deserve such a blissful life you may ask.

I wonder too as the gumption I had evaporates at the smallest task.

Ten points to the Titanic forces that rent my life asunder,

Made me think life had wilted and waned - lost all of it's colour.

What good amidst these forces when my soul is all but spent?

Then I remember the nursery to build, and the seven veils that rent

As ten points the Seers all won for the exemplary lives they led,

Standing tall and battling those titans who over time had fled.

Am I an Aurobindonian, yes, but I do not feel overstrong,

Yet when I pass, the Devil's brood feels as if their time will not last long.
