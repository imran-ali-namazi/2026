Nonviolent Communication (NVC), as formulated by Dr. Marshall Rosenberg, begins fundamentally with **Self-Empathy** (self-connection). You cannot offer pure, uncorrupted empathy to another person if your own internal cup is full of unacknowledged pain, self-judgment, or unrecognized needs. When we skip self-connection, our "attempts to help" or "connect" with others degenerate into subtle demands, passive aggression, or savior dynamics—because we are unconsciously seeking to have our own unmet needs validated through them.

By starting with self-empathy, you translate your internal chatter (the "jackal talk" of blame, guilt, and moralistic judgment) into basic human feelings and universal needs. Self-empathy allows you to notice when your nervous system is dysregulated, preventing reactive defense mechanisms and ensuring that your outreach stems from genuine presence rather than anxiety or a need for control.
