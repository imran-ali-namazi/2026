#### **Chapter 7: The Higher Lines of Karma**

As a human being grows spiritually, the laws governing their Karma change. Aurobindo introduces the idea that mechanical, physical laws of cause and effect give way to moral and spiritual laws. #### **Chapter 8: Karma, Will and Consequence**

Aurobindo addresses the apparent conflict between human free will and cosmic determinism. He argues that our internal _will_ is a real, formative power.
